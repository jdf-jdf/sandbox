Fifteen-minute med checks mean the note usually gets written between patients or after the last one. That's where E/M level slips, either down (lost revenue) or up (audit risk).

JotPsych listens during the visit and drafts the note with the elements that support your coding: the history you actually took, the risk you discussed, time spent. It surfaces suggested CPT and ICD-10 codes for you to confirm or change. The coding call stays yours.

For a group practice, the useful question is whether your levels currently match what your documentation would hold up to. Happy to walk through what the drafts look like on a real panel of med-management visits, 15 minutes.
