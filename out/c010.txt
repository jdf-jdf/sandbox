Splitting time between a clinic and a private caseload usually means two documentation standards, two rhythms, and notes that pile up until 9pm.

JotPsych is an ambient scribe built for behavioral health. It listens during session and drafts a narrative progress note in your language, not a checkbox summary. You review and edit every note before anything is finalized. The clinical thinking stays yours.

Worth saying plainly: it's HIPAA compliant, recordings aren't retained, and you control what gets captured. Most therapists I talk to want that answered first.
