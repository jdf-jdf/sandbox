Couples work is hard to write up. Two people, a shared dynamic, and a progress note that has to capture what happened between them without turning into a transcript. That is not a five-minute note, and it usually waits until after your last session.

JotPsych listens during session and drafts the narrative note in your language. You edit it, you decide what belongs in the record, and nothing goes anywhere until you sign it. It was built for behavioral health specifically, not adapted from a medical scribe.
